RISTORANTE AURELIA – A JEWEL OF ITALIAN LUXURY

Nestled in the heart of the city’s most elegant district, Ristorante Aurelia is where Italy’s timeless charm meets the sophistication of modern fine dining. Named after the ancient Roman road Via Aurelia, which once connected Rome to the Ligurian coast, the restaurant embodies a journey through Italy’s most treasured culinary regions — each plate a destination, each flavor a memory.From the moment you step inside, you are embraced by an atmosphere of understated opulence. The interiors feature hand-carved walnut paneling, Carrara marble accents, and floor-to-ceiling windows that bathe the dining room in golden light. Fresh flowers from the Tuscan countryside adorn each table, while the gentle notes of a live mandolin set the tone for an evening of indulgence.The menu, curated by Executive Chef Lorenzo Bianchi, is a masterful blend of tradition and innovation. Signature dishes include saffron-infused risotto crowned with lobster from the Amalfi Coast, truffle-laced tagliolini made fresh each morning, and slow-roasted lamb inspired by the rustic kitchens of Abruzzo. Every ingredient is sourced with uncompromising care — from organic heirloom tomatoes grown in Campania to artisanal cheeses aged in the caves of Lombardy.
